# Staff guide — Coco Tamarind dashboard

Sign in at **cocotamarindhp27.co.uk/admin** with your own email and password. Never share an account — every change is recorded under your name in the activity log.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Super Admin** | Everything, including staff accounts |
| **Restaurant Manager** | Everything except staff accounts |
| **Reservation Staff** | Bookings, guests (view), enquiries |
| **Content Editor** | Menu, offers, website text, gallery, search titles |

If you see "You don't have access to that page", ask a Super Admin.

## Daily: bookings

**Overview** shows today's bookings and covers, anything waiting for approval, new enquiries and email problems.

**Reservations** shows one day by default. Use *‹ Prev / Today / Next ›*, or search by name, phone, email or reference (e.g. `CT-7KQ4M2`). **Calendar** shows a month at a glance.

- **Phone or walk-in booking:** *New booking* → choose date, guests and time (full times are marked "— full") → leave *Table* on "Assign automatically" or pick one → name and phone (email optional, needed for a confirmation) → *Create booking*. The system won't let two bookings share a table at the same time.
- **Approve a large-party request:** open it → *Confirm*. The guest is emailed.
- **Change a booking:** open it → *Edit booking* → change date, time, guests or table → *Save changes*. Tick "Email the guest" to send them the update.
- **Guest asked for a change** (blue box at the top): make the change, tick *Mark change request handled*, save.
- **Cancel:** *Cancel booking…* → add a reason (it goes in the guest's email) → confirm. Untick nothing if you want the guest emailed; tick *Don't email the guest* if you've already spoken to them.
- **After service:** mark bookings *Completed* or *No-show*. This keeps the reports accurate.
- **Guest notes** (right-hand panel) are kept for their future visits — preferences, regular table, etc.
- **Export CSV** downloads the bookings currently shown.

## Menu

**Menu** lists every category and dish. Changes appear on the website immediately.

- 👁 makes a dish unavailable for the day (it shows as "Unavailable") — click again to bring it back.
- ★ adds it to *Signature dishes* on the homepage (up to 6 show).
- ↑ ↓ reorder dishes and categories.
- Click a dish name to edit name, price, description, category, spice level, vegetarian, photo and allergens.
- **Allergens:** tick every allergen the dish contains, then — only once the head chef has checked it — tick **Allergen information verified**. Until then the website tells guests to ask. Gluten-free can only be set on verified dishes.
- **Desserts** and **Drinks** categories exist but are hidden. Add dishes, then *Edit* the category and tick *Show on website*.
- *Deleted dishes* lets you restore anything removed by mistake.

## Offers

*New offer* → title, type, description, terms (required), optional start and end dates, optional button. Offers go live on their start date and disappear after their end date automatically. *Pause* hides an offer without deleting it.

## Website content

- **Contact details, links & opening note** — phone numbers, email, address, ordering link, review links, parking text. Used everywhere on the site and in search results.
- **Page text** — headings and paragraphs on the homepage, about, celebrations and the menu's allergy notice. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- **Guest testimonials** — only genuine comments you have permission to publish. Leave empty to show review links only.
- **Search engine titles & descriptions** — what Google shows. Keep titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 160.

## Gallery

Upload a photo (or paste a Cloudinary link), describe what's in it (this is read aloud to blind visitors and used by Google), choose a category. Use *Order* numbers to arrange; untick *Show on website* to hide without deleting. The first **Food** photo appears in the homepage arch.

## Enquiries

Event and celebration enquiries, and contact-form messages. Set the status as you work (*New → In progress → Responded → Closed*) and keep internal notes — the guest never sees them. Mark junk as *Spam*.

## Customers

Everyone who has booked with an email. Open a guest to see their history and marketing consent.

- **Record consent** only when the guest has asked to receive marketing; **Record withdrawal** if they ask to stop.
- **Download their data** when a guest asks what we hold about them (send them the file).
- **Erase personal data** when a guest asks to be forgotten — type ERASE to confirm. Booking numbers stay for reports, without their details. Cancel any upcoming bookings first.
- *Export CSV → marketing opt-ins* is the only list that may be used for marketing emails.

## Booking settings (managers)

- **Booking rules** — slot interval, last booking time, notice, how far ahead, table time by party size, largest online party, instant-confirm limit, kitchen pacing, reminder timing. *Accept online bookings* off pauses online booking (the page asks guests to call).
- **Opening hours** and **Closed dates** (e.g. Christmas Day, private events) — the website and booking times update straight away.
- **Seating plan** — your tables. Each table has min/max guests; tables with the same *join group* and *joinable* ticked can be pushed together for larger groups. Untick *in use* to take a table out without deleting it.
- **Integrations** shows whether email, image storage and analytics are configured.
- **Recent emails** shows what was sent; *Retry* resends a failed one.

## Reports

Bookings per week, busiest times and days, party sizes, how far ahead people book, booking sources, enquiries and website actions (booking conversion, order clicks, phone taps). Choose dates at the top; *Export CSV* for spreadsheets. Dish sales and offer revenue live in your Just Order Online account.

## Staff (Super Admin)

*Add a staff member* creates an account and shows a temporary password once — give it to them in person. Change roles, deactivate leavers (they're signed out everywhere), reset passwords or remove two-step verification for someone who has lost their phone.

## My account

Change your password (12+ characters, letters and numbers). Managers and admins should turn on **two-step verification** with an authenticator app.
